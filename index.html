The Learnly method

Four stops from curious to qualified

Every learner walks the same road — but the content on it is shaped around your goal and pace.

Step 01

Pick your path

A 60-second skill quiz maps a route from where you are to the job or project you want.

Step 02

Learn by doing

Twelve-minute lessons, then a hands-on build. Mentors review your work within 24 hours.

Step 03

Build with people

Join a weekly cohort pod and ship a real product with three or four classmates.

Step 04

Prove it

Earn a shareable certificate and get matched with our hiring partners for interviews.
